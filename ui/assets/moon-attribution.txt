AstroApex lunar globe

Maps: NASA's Scientific Visualization Studio, Ernie Wright.
Source: CGI Moon Kit https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
Data: Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera (LROC WAC) and Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA).

The embedded 2048 by 1024 JPEG is the 2025 NASA visualization color map:
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/lroc_color_2k.jpg
The color product includes adjusted exposure and lower-resolution laser albedo at high latitudes.

The source LOLA elevation grid is 1440 by 720 floating-point samples (4 pixels/degree):
https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a000000/a004700/a004720/ldem_4.tif
Heights are kilometers above a reference radius of 1737.4 km. The Blender mesh uses
true-scale bilinear radial displacement on 144 longitude sectors and 72 latitude
intervals. A 1024 by 512 tangent-space normal map derived from DEM gradients adds
finer shading relief. Polar vertices use longitudinally averaged elevations.

Longitude increases eastward. Image top is lunar north. The prime meridian is
at the center of the equirectangular map. In the exported glTF coordinate frame,
+Y is north; +Z points to (0 N, 0 E); +X points to (0 N, 90 E).
The radius of the reference sphere is one scene unit.

This is a generalized global visualization. Its resolution cannot characterize a
landing pad, individual local obstructions, or precise site horizon profiles.
Photometric adjustments, display lighting and normal shading are illustrative.
